**Infosys Ltd. – Integrated Financial Modeling & Valuation Project**

Developed a comprehensive financial model and valuation report for Infosys Ltd. covering historical financial performance from FY2022–FY2026 and financial projections for FY2027–FY2031. The project applies financial modeling, corporate valuation, and investment analysis techniques used in equity research and investment banking.

**Key Project Highlights:**

- **Three-Statement Financial Modeling:** Built integrated Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement projections using historical financial data and forecasting assumptions.
- **Financial Forecasting:** Projected revenue, operating expenses, EBITDA, EBIT, net profit, EPS, working capital, capital expenditure, and retained earnings.
- **Ratio & Common-Size Analysis:** Evaluated profitability, liquidity-related indicators, operational efficiency, leverage, growth trends, and financial statement composition.
- **Beta & WACC Calculation:** Estimated equity beta using regression analysis and calculated the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) to support valuation.
- **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation:** Estimated free cash flows, terminal value, enterprise value, equity value, and implied share price, incorporating sensitivity analysis.
- **Comparable Company Analysis (CCA):** Benchmarked Infosys against industry peers using EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, and P/E valuation multiples.
- **Football Field Valuation:** Compared valuation ranges from DCF scenarios, comparable company analysis, and the 52-week share price range.
- **DuPont Analysis:** Analysed Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Assets (ROA) through profitability, asset efficiency, and financial leverage.
- **Investment Research & Presentation:** Prepared a company overview, financial summary, investment outlook, key business risks, and valuation assessment.

**Key Skills Demonstrated:** Financial Modeling, Financial Statement Analysis, DCF Valuation, Comparable Company Analysis, WACC, Beta Regression, Ratio Analysis, Financial Forecasting, Sensitivity Analysis, DuPont Analysis, and Excel-Based Financial Analysis.

*This project was developed for educational and analytical purposes and does not constitute investment advice.*
