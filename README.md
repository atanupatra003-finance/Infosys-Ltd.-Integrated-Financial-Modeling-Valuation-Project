# Infosys Ltd. – Financial Modeling & Valuation Project

## Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive financial modeling and valuation analysis of Infosys Ltd., an Indian multinational IT services and consulting company. The model covers historical financial performance from FY2022 to FY2026 and projected financial statements from FY2027 to FY2031.

The objective of this project is to evaluate the company's financial performance, forecast future financial results, estimate intrinsic value using Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) analysis, and compare its valuation with industry peers.

## Key Project Highlights

### 1. Integrated Three-Statement Financial Model
- Developed historical and projected Income Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow Statements.
- Linked key financial statements through forecasting assumptions.
- Incorporated revenue growth, operating expenses, depreciation, working capital, capital expenditure, and retained earnings.
- Included balance sheet integrity checks.

### 2. Financial Statement & Ratio Analysis
- Analysed historical revenue, profitability, earnings, and cash flow trends.
- Evaluated EBITDA margin, EBIT margin, net profit margin, ROE, ROA, and other financial ratios.
- Prepared common-size income statement and balance sheet analysis.
- Performed DuPont analysis to understand the key drivers of Return on Equity (ROE).

### 3. Beta & WACC Calculation
- Estimated equity beta using regression analysis.
- Applied the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) to estimate the cost of equity.
- Calculated the after-tax cost of debt and Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).
- Used WACC as the discount rate for DCF valuation.

### 4. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation
- Forecast free cash flows for FY2027–FY2031.
- Estimated terminal value using the perpetual growth and exit-multiple approaches.
- Calculated enterprise value, equity value, and implied share price.
- Incorporated sensitivity analysis to evaluate the impact of WACC and terminal growth assumptions on valuation.

### 5. Comparable Company Analysis (CCA)
- Benchmarked Infosys against TCS, HCL Technologies, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, and LTIMindtree.
- Applied EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, and P/E multiples.
- Estimated implied valuation ranges based on peer-group trading multiples.

### 6. Football Field Valuation
- Compared valuation outcomes from comparable-company analysis and DCF scenarios.
- Included bear-case, base-case, and bull-case DCF ranges.
- Presented valuation ranges alongside the reported 52-week share-price range.
